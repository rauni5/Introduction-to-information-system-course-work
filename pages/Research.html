Shop

Caliber Nepal Shop

Akira Shop

The Shop page is designed to be minimalist and user-friendly, allowing customers to effortlessly browse and discover products. For inspiration, we looked to Caliber Nepal—a website we've frequently used for shoe purchases and always admired for its user-friendly interface. Their clean product grids influenced our design approach. For Akira’s Shop, we incorporated these functional elements while adding our own creative touches.
